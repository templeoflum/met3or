MET∃OR · SURFACE STUDIES

One mechanism each, isolated so it can be judged on its own — the same form as the toy studies, turned on the surface instead of on an instrument. None of these is a page. Each is a single behaviour, built far enough to be felt and no further.

Each is self-contained — no build, no dependency, one file.
Which means any of them can drop straight into gallery/ as a lore module and mount on the console with no ceremony.

S-01DWELLthe ethos, as physics
Legibility is a function of how still you are. Move and the mark dissolves; rest and it resolves; hold 2.5 s and it blooms. Clicking costs you.
twist — the free-run bloom made the only way in. A page that pays out for the one behaviour attention design punishes.
S-02CARRIAGEnothing ships plain
All text is carried in balanced ternary, coloured by sign, and decodes only under proximity. You read the page the way you read an instrument — close, and one part at a time.
twist — CIPHER.md applied to the website itself. The page looks like notation until you lean in.
S-03BOUSTROPHEDONtypography
Alternate lines are written in the return direction, and the sweep decides how far the return has turned. At the seam they are edge-on — legible to neither world.
twist — the name's own etymology as the page's typesetting. Unreadable on purpose, which is the point.
S-04PHOSPHORthe felt quality
The same motion twice, side by side: a conventional easing curve against the engine's own decay — half-life .12 s — with everything breathing at f₀ 1.2 Hz.
twist — the house physics as the house style. The difference isn't visible; it's felt. Nothing snaps, everything settles.
S-05SWEEPnavigation · boldest
No scroll, ever. θ is the only axis, and destinations are positions you arrive at rather than links in a row. Drag to travel; a station opens on arrival.
twist — navigation as tuning rather than choosing. The most dangerous idea here: beautiful, and it can cost you the routing.
S-06REGISTERframing · anti-metric
The page is a filed document. Registered items take measures and dispositions; one row has neither and cannot be given one. The counter counts in base 3 and reads 000 forever.
twist — absence as the mark. The subject of the document is the row that isn't in it — and the page states, as a readout, that it measures nothing.
S-07ANOMALYthe secret
A layout whose mirror symmetry is real, broken in exactly one place. Five letters reflect honestly about the datum. R does not.
twist — two mirrors composed are a direct isometry, so R cannot arrive backwards — C₁, the only letter with no symmetry of its own. Unrewarded except by noticing.
Studies are not proposals — they isolate a mechanic so it can be kept, combined or thrown out on its own merits.
S-04 + S-01 + S-02 compound well: the feel, the ethos, the surface. S-05 is the one that trades routing for art.
