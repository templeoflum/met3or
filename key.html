Value: 9 × first + 3 × second + last, in reading order. Character range: A–M, +1 to +13; N–Z, −1 to −13. Space: 000.

Reading order: boustrophedon. Row 1 reads every sign left to right →. Row 2 reads every sign right to left ←, including the signs within each triple. Alternate thereafter. Words may cross row boundaries; triples may not.

000 encodes a space. Row breaks encode nothing. Full stops remain literal: .. A return row reverses the full stop with the signs; decoding restores its position.

02 / transcription

Enter one physical row per line. Preserve row order and symbol order. Stored signals: A, B. The optional KEY / ++ header is excluded from the signal.

Accepted input: +, 0, −, the listed equivalents, and literal full stops. Typed spaces are ignored; word spaces require 000; row breaks add no characters. Each new line reverses the complete sign sequence. Long rows scroll horizontally.

03 / conjugation

Polarity reversal: + ↔ −; 0 unchanged. Character substitution: A ↔ N, B ↔ O, through M ↔ Z (ROT13). Two reversals restore the original sequence. Space remains 000.
