Read a triple as 9 × first + 3 × second + last. A–M occupy +1 to +13; N–Z occupy −1 to −13. 000 is a space.

Start on the top row, left to right →. On the next row, read right to left ←, including the signs within each triple. Alternate at every row. This is boustrophedon.

Keep each row intact. A turn adds no space: a space is always its own 000 triple, even at the end of a row.

02 / recover

Transcribe the signs as they sit on the page, one physical row per line. Or load a carried signal. Nothing decodes until you ask.

Use +, 0, − or their costumes. Spaces separate triples; new lines turn the reading direction. Long rows scroll sideways.

03 / mirror

Swap + and −; keep 0. Each letter moves thirteen places: A ↔ N, B ↔ O, through M ↔ Z. This is ROT13. Mirror twice to return. Spaces remain spaces.
